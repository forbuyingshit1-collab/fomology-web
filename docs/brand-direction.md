# FOMOLOGY Brand Direction

FOMOLOGY is Thai-first, editorial, creator-confident, and led by real work rather than feature grids.

## Professional trust narrative (confirmed 2026-09-24)

- Reframe the homepage around a business or team that wants trusted help with a content or operations problem. The commissioning client becomes the primary audience; Course and Lab remain useful but secondary paths.
- Present FOMOLOGY as an **Expert Studio**: compact, hands-on, and able to take responsibility from clarifying the problem through design and delivery. Do not imply a large agency team or reduce the brand to a solo-freelancer persona.
- Build authority from real work, the role FOMOLOGY performed, the working process, honest maturity labels, and carefully attributed outcomes. Avoid generic claims such as “highly experienced,” unsupported years or project counts, and geography as a shortcut to credibility.
- Remove South Korea and other country labels from the client-work trust narrative. Project relevance and delivered responsibility should carry the proof.
- Lead the Hero with experience gained from real work, then connect that experience to the client's project. Let the work below substantiate the statement rather than using years, project counts, or superlatives.
- Organize commissioned work into two comprehensible pillars with Thai headings and restrained English labels: **สื่อและประสบการณ์ดิจิทัล** / `CONTENT & DIGITAL EXPERIENCE` for AI video and websites, and **ระบบและ Automation** / `SYSTEMS & AUTOMATION` for custom systems, integrations, and workflows.
- Describe the studio engagement as understanding the problem, designing the approach, building the work, and delivering it for the agreed use. Do not imply default unlimited maintenance.
- Structure each client-work proof around the problem, FOMOLOGY's responsibility, and what was delivered. Do not replace country labels with a decorative technology list.
- Keep Course and Lab as a substantial **Learn & Create** chapter after the service-and-proof story. It can carry visual weight without becoming the opening protagonist.
- Use the homepage sequence Hero → client-work proof → two service pillars → studio process → Learn & Create → final inquiry. Open with “จากประสบการณ์ที่ลงมือทำจริง เราช่วยเปลี่ยนโจทย์ของคุณให้เป็นงานที่พร้อมใช้” and use “ปรึกษาโปรเจกต์” as the primary inquiry label.
- Retain the Chat Bot capacity and three-month revenue outcomes only as secondary, explicitly client-reported evidence. Never turn them into a general service promise.
- Give Learn & Create visual weight comparable to the service chapter, but keep services first in reading order and “ปรึกษาโปรเจกต์” as the primary conversion. Course and Lab are the alternative path for visitors who want to do the work themselves.
- Apply the narrative across Landing, Products, metadata, navigation, CTAs, and cross-links from Course and Blog. Preserve the owner's personal voice inside Blog article bodies.
- Present the studio process with Thai headings and small English labels: understand the problem, design the approach, build, and deliver.
- Use concise Client Work Stories on Landing and expanded in-page versions on Products. Separate case-study routes are optional and require enough evidence.
- Speak as “เรา” or “FOMOLOGY” without claiming a team size. Replace country metadata on client work with case-specific responsibility labels.
- Consolidate the existing Landing sections: Client Work remains the first proof, the AI-video showcase and recorded audience results move under **สื่อและประสบการณ์ดิจิทัล**, and the old Creator Journey becomes the four-stage studio process.
- Retain the existing AI-video Hero clip. Counter its narrow visual signal with explicit Hero copy and labels covering video, websites, systems, and automation; do not add a heavier autoplay montage.
- Order Products as Hero → expanded Client Work Stories → two service pillars → Independent Work → Learn & Create → final Project Inquiry.
- Keep Messenger as the working destination for “ปรึกษาโปรเจกต์” in this phase.
- While Course and Lab are unavailable, present their intended value with clearly visible `IN DEVELOPMENT` / `PREVIEW` status and no interactive CTA. Never invite a click into a dead end.
- Change the public navigation label from `Products` to `Services` while retaining the `/products/` route.
- Title Previous Client Work “โจทย์จริง งานจริง ส่งมอบจริง” rather than using geography or a generic portfolio heading.
- Use the confirmed Hero copy: “จากประสบการณ์ที่ลงมือทำจริง เราช่วยเปลี่ยนโจทย์ของคุณให้เป็นงานที่พร้อมใช้” with supporting copy about understanding the problem, choosing an approach, and delivering across AI video, websites, custom systems, and automation.
- Preserve the established palette, typography, motion, and visual grammar. This change restructures narrative and copy rather than starting a visual redesign.
- Retain the `MADE WITH FOMOLOGY` title when the video showcase moves under **สื่อและประสบการณ์ดิจิทัล**. Its placement and surrounding scope labels must prevent the showcase from redefining FOMOLOGY as video-only.

## Clean editorial refinement (confirmed 2026-09-22)

- Move the Landing Page and Free Course together toward a quieter editorial system; keep the story and four-color palette, with the blue Course moment as the single strongest visual peak.
- Reduce competing oversized headings and ornamental overlap. Negative space should support reading rather than become another effect.
- Keep display sizes restrained, with comfortable Thai line-height and no aggressive negative letter-spacing. The later, confirmed LINE Seed Sans TH system below supersedes the earlier Noto Sans Thai choice.
- Keep both generated editorial artworks as supporting decoration, but remove the labels and Creator Trace line that currently cross the Hero artwork. The image should have a distinct quiet field; Creator Trace remains elsewhere in the journey.
- Use a Thai-first primary title on the Course page (`ทำวิดีโอแรกให้พร้อมโพสต์`); retain `AI VIDEO FIRST WIN` only as a small English label.
- Condense the six-stage Creator Path on the page into a short bridge from Proof to Course. The full six-stage model remains in the design artifact.
- While lesson content is pending, show one restrained, honest holding state in the Course UI. Keep missing-field and deployment details in `validate:launch`, not repeated in visitor-facing lesson panels.

## Visual grammar

- Use a **Chaptered Editorial** page: title page, separate media chapters, raw evidence, a compact Creator Path bridge, one high-energy Course peak, and a quiet future Lab afterglow.
- Avoid equal card grids, fake dashboards, gradient text, premium-minimal emptiness, and full-bleed media under the hero copy.
- Motion is moderate and scroll-triggered. Use flow entrances, local reveals, the Creator Trace, and small red/blue pixel accents at Landing story transitions and Blog/Products hover or focus states; do not pin whole scenes, run continuously moving backgrounds, or turn the UI into retro pixel art or glitch effects.
- Reduced-motion presents the complete static composition.

## Typography (updated 2026-09-22)

- **LINE Seed Sans TH Bold** for most Thai headings; use **ExtraBold** sparingly on short standout phrases, with scale chosen to preserve the quieter editorial hierarchy.
- **LINE Seed Sans TH Regular** for Thai body copy, navigation, buttons, captions, and controls.
- **IBM Plex Mono Medium** for English labels and dates, tracked `0.15em`–`0.25em`; never apply that spacing to Thai body text.
- **Anton** only for the FOMOLOGY wordmark and restrained numeric accents, not labels, dates, or dominant content headlines.
- Apply the system across Landing, Course, Blog, and Products. No serif or pixel font; “pixel” refers to motion accents, not typography.

## Four-color system

- Canvas: `#F7F7F5` — every light ground and inverse text.
- Ink: `#17181B` — body text, rules, dark evidence field, and production CTA.
- Signal Red: `#FF3131` — unresolved idea, narrative markers, proof emphasis, pending and Coming Soon.
- Electric Blue: `#2E2BFF` — interactive Course path, primary CTA, completion, and First Win.
- Opacity variants may be derived from these four colors. Do not introduce white, yellow, or additional source colors in UI styling.

## Signature move

**Creator Trace** begins as a red idea mark, resolves into a blue Course path and First Win, then points to a separate future Lab project. Red never implies the Course action; blue never implies unavailable Lab interaction.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop uses asymmetric editorial spreads and a concise bridge that summarizes the six-stage journey.
- Mobile turns evidence into a native horizontal rail and stacks each Showcase chapter without reducing it to a small card.
- Navigation keeps Course visibly interactive on desktop and mobile; Lab remains visibly unavailable. Messenger is secondary on mobile.
- Once Blog and Products are added, mobile navigation keeps Course directly visible while Blog, Products, and Messenger sit in an accessible menu.
- Tap targets remain at least 44px and essential Thai copy stays readable.
