# FOMOLOGY Mobile Experience Brief

## Goal

Give mobile visitors the same story, proof, and brand character as desktop without shrinking the desktop composition into a narrow viewport. Mobile uses the same chapter order with a faster initial scan and progressive disclosure for repeated detail.

## Navigation

- Apply the mobile navigation pattern across every public page.
- Keep a compact menu trigger at the left of the sticky top header through the full mobile range.
- Keep the square FOMOLOGY logo centered in the mobile header.
- Open navigation as a left drawer over the current page with a backdrop; never push or resize page content.
- Include Home, Services, Blog, Course, `Lab — Soon`, and Project Inquiry.
- The drawer must close on route selection, backdrop selection, and Escape, and must expose accurate expanded/collapsed state to assistive technology.

## Condensed Mobile Story

- Preserve every homepage chapter and the desktop narrative order.
- Keep section headings and introductions visible.
- Collapse repeated detail inside client-work and service cards by default.
- Expand details inline in the existing card; do not navigate away or open a second overlay.
- A client-work card always shows its video preview, project title, responsibility, and strongest supported outcome. Process and secondary scope sit behind `ดูเพิ่มเติม`.
- A service card always shows its image, label, title, and concise summary. Deliverables and availability notes sit behind `ดูเพิ่มเติม`.
- Desktop and full Services-page presentations remain unchanged unless they already enter the mobile breakpoint.

## Video Previews

- Keep the Hero preview portrait and cap it around 300–320px on phones and 360–400px on tablets. It must not grow larger on tablet than on desktop.
- Keep client-work previews in a horizontal snap rail, with supporting detail condensed rather than shrinking the media excessively.
- Present `Made with FOMOLOGY` as a horizontal snap rail on mobile, with a maximum media width around 320px and a hint of the next card visible.
- At most one prominent preview plays at a time. Pause it when it leaves the viewport.
- Respect Reduced Motion by keeping previews on their posters rather than autoplaying.
- The full-video dialog retains native controls and the existing contained mobile layout.

## Readability

- Keep body text at least 16–17px with approximately 1.65–1.75 line height.
- Keep mobile section headings around 32–40px unless a shorter display word needs a deliberate exception.
- Reduce tracking on long mono labels so they wrap as readable phrases.
- Remove `nowrap` only where it creates narrow-screen pressure; retain it for short intentional phrase groups.
- Keep the smallest interactive target at least 44px.

## Responsive Boundaries

- The top-header mobile navigation and condensed card behaviors apply through 700px.
- From 701px through 900px, retain the existing header pattern while applying the smaller video caps and appropriate typography.
- Keep the same top-header trigger below 390px so narrow screens retain their full reading width.

## Non-goals

- A separate mobile site or alternate content hierarchy.
- Removing homepage chapters.
- Making every video preview the same size.
- Letting the drawer push the page sideways.
- Changing desktop navigation or desktop card density.
