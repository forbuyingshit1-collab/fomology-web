# FOMOLOGY Professional Trust Story — Working Brief

Status: implemented and production-build verified on 2026-09-24.

## Positioning

- The primary homepage audience is a business or team that wants trusted help with a content or operations problem.
- FOMOLOGY presents itself as an **Expert Studio**: compact, hands-on, and responsible from understanding the problem through delivery.
- Professional trust comes from real work, clear responsibility, process, honest maturity labels, and carefully attributed outcomes—not geography, unsupported scale, or generic expertise claims.
- Site-wide brand copy speaks as “เรา” or “FOMOLOGY” without claiming team size. Personal Blog bodies keep the owner's original voice.
