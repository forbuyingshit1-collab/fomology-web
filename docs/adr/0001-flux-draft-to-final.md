# Preserve creative direction with FLUX Draft to Final

FOMOLOGY will render an approved FLUX 3 Draft into a FLUX 3 Final FHD video so the composition and motion remain consistent with what the creator selected. Topaz Astra 2 is not the default final-render step because it is a creative upscaler that may invent details; it will instead be offered as an optional 4K Enhance for creators who want a premium transformation.
