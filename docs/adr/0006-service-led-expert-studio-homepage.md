# Lead with the Expert Studio while preserving Learn & Create

FOMOLOGY will reframe the homepage from a Course-led Creator Journey into a service-led Expert Studio story for businesses and teams: experience from real work is followed by client proof, two service pillars, an end-to-end studio process, and a project inquiry. Course and Lab remain a substantial, comparably sized Learn & Create path for visitors who want to do the work themselves, but services retain first reading order and the primary conversion; the usable Free Course remains a public-launch requirement from ADR 0005. This direction was chosen because delivered work, clear responsibility, and process provide stronger professional trust than geography, unsupported scale claims, or an equal-audience opening.
