# Launch with a free course and creator showcase

Phase 1 will lead with Made with FOMOLOGY as the homepage hero for both learners and prospective Production Clients, and use a low-pressure Contact Us action to Facebook Messenger; it will not display service packages or pricing. The Free Course builds trust and an audience, while FOMOLOGY Lab appears as Coming Soon. Generation, credits, top-ups, and the paid course are deferred to Phase 2 so the launch does not depend on operating a costly AI-video platform before demand is proven.
