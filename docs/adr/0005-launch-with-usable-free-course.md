# Launch with a usable Free Course while Lab remains a preview

FOMOLOGY will hold its public launch until the ungated, single-page Free Course is ready to take a Primary Creator through three short, video-led stages to a First Win in one sitting; each stage also provides a written summary and checklist. The Landing Page and Course will launch together, with device-local progress and no account, while FOMOLOGY Lab remains a non-interactive preview. This replaces the showcase-first launch so the Creator Journey leads somewhere real without forcing the project to build Lab, authentication, payments, or backend infrastructure at the same time.
