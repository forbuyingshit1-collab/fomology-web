# FOMOLOGY Course-Ready Landing Brief

Interviewed and resolved with the project owner on 2026-09-21. Statements below marked as confirmed decisions summarize the accepted grilling session; they are not invented quotations.

## 1. Vibe and references

**Evidence from the existing brand direction:** Thai-first, creator-confident, editorial, playful, accessible. The visual reference is FOMOLOGY's own supplied video work and restrained pixel identity rather than another website.

## 2. Scroll journey

**Confirmed decision:** A Primary Creator arrives with an idea but no clear starting point. They see what AI video can become, see real results, discover a usable Free Course, reach a First Win, and then see FOMOLOGY Lab as the future path for a new project. A Production Service inquiry is a secondary route at the end.

## 3. Energy curve

The opening is spacious and direct. The three Showcase Clips increase visual energy one chapter at a time. Results become quieter and evidential. The Course reveal is the one high-energy peak. Lab settles into forward-looking anticipation. The close is calm and decisive.

## 4. Feeling curve and peak

1. **Recognition:** The title page says an idea can become a video and addresses the Creator directly.
2. **Curiosity:** Three distinct Thai-first video worlds arrive as separate editorial chapters.
3. **Trust:** Raw analytics evidence follows the work without redesigning the screenshots.
4. **Clarity:** A compact path separates the Free Course First Win from a future Lab Project.
5. **Confidence:** The Course promise lands: a first post-ready video in 15 minutes. This is the engineered peak and gets the largest visual change and strongest action.
6. **Anticipation:** Lab shows New Idea → Draft → Final → optional 4K Enhance as a future path, clearly unavailable today.
7. **Resolve:** The Creator can start the Course; a Production Client can instead open Messenger.

**Peak sentence:** “The page showed me I could make my first post-ready AI video in 15 minutes, then gave me the course to do it.”

## 5. Signature move

**Creator Trace:** A thin editorial trace accumulates down the page as the visitor passes each chapter. It begins as an unresolved red idea mark, resolves into an electric-blue Course path at the peak, and forks only after the First Win to label the separate future Lab Project. It never replaces headings or navigation and becomes static under reduced motion.

**Tell-someone sentence:** “It is the site where your loose idea becomes a clear blue path to a first video, then opens a separate route for the next one.”

## 6. Aesthetic range

**Confirmed direction:** Editorial rather than premium-minimal. Predominantly Canvas and Ink with Signal Red for narrative/proof and Electric Blue for interactive Course actions. Only these four source colors and their opacity variants are allowed.

## 7. World and grammar

**Grammar:** Chaptered editorial.

**World:** High-key editorial using the supplied real footage and screenshots. No generated assets, no clay world, no fake dashboard, no full-frame media under the title-page copy.

The page uses hard chapter boundaries, asymmetric spreads, media in its own column, and a non-fixed masthead. It does not use continuous color drift, a full-bleed scrub hero, pinned crossfade type acts, spotlight, or magnetic CTAs.

## 8. Existing assets

- Official FOMOLOGY logo and Anton wordmark
- Three Cloudinary-hosted Showcase Clips with posters and muted previews
- Three raw Cloudinary-hosted analytics screenshots
- Anton and Noto Sans Thai via `next/font`
- Existing Facebook Messenger, Facebook, and TikTok destinations

## Belief and action

**Belief to install:** “I have a clear, approachable path from my idea to a first AI video.”

**Primary action:** `เริ่มคอร์สฟรี` → `/course`

**Secondary action:** `ติดต่อเรา` → Facebook Messenger

## Scroll score

| Beat | Device family | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Flow + restrained kinetic type | A title page should read immediately before media begins. |
| Curiosity | Reveal + local parallax | Each supplied clip becomes a distinct chapter without turning into a card grid. |
| Trust | Native evidence rail + flow entrance | Raw screenshots stay intact and readable. |
| Clarity | Bespoke Creator Trace | The distinction between First Win and a new Lab Project becomes visible. |
| Confidence (peak) | Count + large editorial field | The real 15-minute promise gets the strongest contrast and most room. |
| Anticipation | Process line + reveal | Lab communicates a future sequence without pretending to be usable. |
| Resolve | Flow close | The ending holds two honest next steps and does not fade into the footer. |

## Fingerprint gate

The registry is empty, so this first build clears the gate automatically. Planned fingerprint: chaptered editorial; non-fixed masthead; typographic title page; flow → reveal/parallax → evidence rail → bespoke trace → count peak → process reveal → flow close; colophon close; Creator Trace signature move.

## Authored silence

The evidence section deliberately removes decorative motion so the raw screenshots can be examined. This quiet is intentional and must not be treated as dead scroll.
