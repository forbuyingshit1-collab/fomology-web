# FOMOLOGY

FOMOLOGY presents real work and its recorded outcomes, with equal paths for learning through courses and commissioning work through Anantasak Charoensuk's Fastwork profile.

## Language

**Primary Creator**:
A Thai creator aged 40 or older who wants to make short-form video with AI but is not comfortable with technical tools.
_Avoid_: elderly user, non-tech user, general creator

**Creator Journey**:
The learning journey from curiosity through the teacher's actual work to an available course or relevant field note. The Free Course provides the first guided entry; commissioned work is a separate Fastwork destination.
_Avoid_: guaranteed learner outcome, prerequisite for commissioning

**Work & Learn Homepage**:
The FOMOLOGY entrance led by actual cases and recorded outcomes. Visitors can choose learning or commissioning with equal emphasis; courses live on FOMOLOGY and project inquiries go to Fastwork.
_Avoid_: course-primary homepage, service-primary homepage, marketplace

**Featured Case**:
A specific piece of original work presented with its purpose and an attributable outcome or delivery. A recorded reach claim belongs to its confirmed work; aggregate account results remain separate evidence.
_Avoid_: generic capability slide, unassigned success metric, guaranteed result

**Practice Explorer**:
The selectable view of FOMOLOGY's actual AI Video, Websites, and Automation work. Each practice pairs its work with an honest learning next step. Work by the teacher demonstrates possibilities and is distinct from learner outcomes.
_Avoid_: Fastwork embed, learner portfolio, marketplace profile

**Fastwork Hiring Path**:
The external destination where a visitor can explore Anantasak Charoensuk's commissioned work and discuss a project. It is separate from learning and course discovery on FOMOLOGY.
_Avoid_: course checkout, FOMOLOGY account, on-site inquiry

**First Win**:
The Primary Creator installs the Prompt Skill, turns a written idea into a directed prompt, and generates and downloads a post-ready vertical FLUX.3 video of 8–10 seconds with audio. The first guided setup takes 30–45 minutes; later idea-to-generation runs target about 15 minutes.
_Avoid_: first generation, demo output, test render

**Guided Trial**:
The primary onboarding path from the Made with FOMOLOGY showcase into creating a first video with step-by-step guidance. The free manual is a secondary path, not a prerequisite.
_Avoid_: free demo, manual funnel

**Made with FOMOLOGY**:
The curated AI-video showcase retained under the Content & Digital Experience pillar with its existing English title. It demonstrates motion work FOMOLOGY can produce without defining the studio as video-only.
_Avoid_: gallery, portfolio, feed

**Showcase Clip**:
A Cloudinary-hosted video presented at its approximately 4:3 source ratio, with a short muted preview loop inside Made with FOMOLOGY and the full video available on selection; TikTok remains a separate viewing destination.
_Avoid_: TikTok embed, social post

**Free Course**:
The ungated, text-first single-page course that takes a Primary Creator through three modules and ten short lessons to a First Win without requiring FOMOLOGY Lab. It uses optional deeper explanations and real examples while keeping one beginner path clear.
_Avoid_: lead magnet, free manual

**FLUX.3 First Win Course**:
The first Free Course, publicly titled “สร้าง AI Video แรกด้วย FLUX.3,” covering Prompt fundamentals, Codex Skill setup, guided generation, and review.
_Avoid_: advanced prompt engineering course, Custom GPT course, API reference manual

**Part 1 Recording Slides**:
The concise visual companion to the FLUX.3 First Win Course for recording a new spoken explanation. The owner's audio recordings inform its teaching voice and ideas; concept illustrations explain the workflow, while supplied real media show separate teaching cases clearly labeled as distinct from the café Guided Project.
_Avoid_: timed transcription, screenshot simulation, replacement course

**Real Work Insert**:
An owner-supplied image or video placed in the Part 1 Recording Slides to illustrate a teaching point. It keeps its own subject and workflow label, and never stands in as proof that a step of the café Guided Project was completed.
_Avoid_: café exercise output, simulated setup proof, unlabeled case media

**Prompt Skill**:
The downloadable Codex Skill that interviews a Primary Creator, converts a brief into a directed FLUX.3 prompt and payload, validates the request, and supports an explicitly approved generation step.
_Avoid_: prompt template, Custom GPT, website generator

**Skill Package**:
The versioned GitHub Release bundle containing the Prompt Skill, Windows and cross-platform generation helpers, Thai setup guidance, examples, attribution, and release information. It runs locally and never sends an API key through the FOMOLOGY website.
_Avoid_: single SKILL.md download, web generator, installer application

**FLUX.3 Case Library**:
The five owner-supplied videos presented as distinct teaching cases, each linked to the prompt pattern it demonstrates without making its visual style a default.
_Avoid_: template gallery, showcase replacement, prompt preset pack

**Guided Project**:
The original beginner text-to-video exercise used throughout the FLUX.3 First Win Course: an 8–10 second vertical scene in which a middle-aged Thai café owner opens the shop and places one iced coffee in morning light. It has no dialogue, ends on the drink with the owner behind it, and can be personalized without supplying source media.
_Avoid_: showcase recreation, Gold Example, final portfolio piece

**Learn & Create Chapter**:
The learning preview retained on legacy service pages. The current homepage gives the available Free Course its own primary discovery section; a future Lab is separate from the available course.
_Avoid_: unavailable free course, Lab prerequisite, paid enrollment promise

**Course-Ready Launch**:
The public launch boundary at which the Landing Page, a usable Free Course, and substantive Blog and Products pages ship together while FOMOLOGY Lab remains a non-interactive preview.
_Avoid_: showcase-first launch, Course teaser launch

**Blog**:
FOMOLOGY's editorial archive of the owner's writing on AI, automation, technology, practical learning, and related experiments, including the existing articles brought under the FOMOLOGY name. It can be broader than the Creator Journey on the homepage.
_Avoid_: company news feed, fabricated case study, course lesson

**Products Page**:
The service-and-proof page ordered as an experience-led Hero, expanded Client Work Stories, the two service pillars, maturity-labeled Independent Work, Learn & Create preview, and a final Project Inquiry.
_Avoid_: storefront, fixed-price catalog, checkout

**Legacy Service-Led Homepage**:
The previous story for a business or team seeking commissioned work, where delivery proof and service capabilities preceded learning. It describes historical design context rather than the current Work & Learn Homepage.
_Avoid_: current homepage, Work & Learn Homepage

**Expert Studio**:
FOMOLOGY's public identity as a compact, hands-on studio that clarifies problems, designs an approach, and delivers the work. It does not imply a large agency team or present the brand as a solo freelancer.
_Avoid_: full-service agency, large team, solo freelancer

**Experience Proof**:
Trust demonstrated through real work, the responsibility FOMOLOGY took, the process used, accurate maturity labels, and carefully attributed outcomes. It does not rely on unsupported years, project counts, expertise claims, or geography.
_Avoid_: trusted by, highly experienced, international credibility, unsupported metrics

**Experience-Led Hero**:
The previous service-led opening that reframed the FOMO acronym and introduced broad studio capabilities with proof shortcuts and a video. It remains historical design vocabulary; the current homepage opens with a learning invitation.
_Avoid_: From Vision to Action, expert-studio label, highly experienced, industry-leading, trusted expert

**Proof Shortcut**:
The equal pair of Hero links “สนใจ AI Video” and “สนใจ Automation” that takes visitors directly to matching proof on the same page. AI Video lands at the `Made with FOMOLOGY` heading; Automation lands at the client-work video heading. The links are evidence shortcuts before the Project Inquiry, not contact actions or service-detail selectors. Their neutral resting state becomes Signal Red for AI Video and Electric Blue for Automation on hover, keyboard focus, or press.
_Avoid_: Service Choice, service package, Messenger CTA, primary and secondary CTA

**Services Navigation**:
The previous navigation label for the retained service archive. Current navigation names the external Fastwork hiring destination explicitly; legacy service pages remain available to visitors following older links.
_Avoid_: Products, Store, Shop

**Hero Proof Clip**:
The existing AI-video clip retained as the Hero visual. Because it represents only one capability, nearby copy and labels must explicitly establish video, websites, systems, and automation as the studio's broader scope.
_Avoid_: brand-wide showreel, proof of every service, autoplay montage

**Content & Digital Experience**:
The service pillar titled “สื่อและประสบการณ์ดิจิทัล” with the English label `CONTENT & DIGITAL EXPERIENCE`, covering AI video and websites that people see and use directly.
_Avoid_: creative services menu, client-facing experience, front-end only

**Systems & Automation**:
The service pillar for operational work behind the experience, including custom systems, integrations, and automated workflows.
_Avoid_: back office only, technical services menu

**Studio Engagement**:
The end-to-end commissioned path: understand the problem, design the approach, build the work, and deliver it ready for its agreed use. Ongoing maintenance is a separate project decision rather than a default promise.
_Avoid_: requirement intake only, unlimited support

**Client Work Story**:
A proof structure for each delivered project: the client's problem, FOMOLOGY's responsibility, and what was delivered. The homepage uses a concise version; Products expands the same evidence in-page, while a separate case study is used only when the available evidence can support it.
_Avoid_: country badge, technology list as proof, unsupported case study

**Responsibility Label**:
The case-specific summary of what FOMOLOGY owned in a client project, replacing country metadata on client-work cards.
_Avoid_: country, industry prestige, decorative technology list

**Experience-Led Story Order**:
The historical service-led sequence, where client proof preceded capability claims. Current discovery follows the Work & Learn Homepage instead.
_Avoid_: capability list before proof, Course-first story

**Condensed Mobile Story**:
The mobile learning entrance keeps the same meaningful sequence as desktop while composing the opening work cards, practice selector, course, reading, and hiring destination for a narrow screen.
_Avoid_: separate mobile site, desktop layout shrunk to fit, removed story chapters, all detail expanded by default

**Mobile Proof Summary**:
The always-visible portion of a mobile client-work card: its preview, title, FOMOLOGY's responsibility, and the strongest supported outcome. Process and scope remain available on request without competing with the initial scan.
_Avoid_: title-only card, full case expanded by default, outcome without attribution

**Mobile Navigation**:
The compact site-wide header with a visible learning action and a menu for course discovery, work, reading, and the external Fastwork hiring path.
_Avoid_: persistent side rail, desktop navigation squeezed into mobile, permanently expanded side menu

**Studio Voice**:
Site-wide brand copy speaks as “เรา” or “FOMOLOGY” without implying team size; Thai headings may carry restrained English labels. Personal Blog articles may retain the owner's original first-person voice.
_Avoid_: team FOMOLOGY, founder persona as site voice, corporate agency voice

**Previous Client Work**:
Real systems delivered for clients, including Chat Bot, StoryUp, SMFI, and RealSun. Selected examples can appear in the Practice Explorer; fuller historical cases remain on Services. This work is distinct from learner outcomes, independently built prototypes, exercises, and Lab experiments.
_Avoid_: all work, portfolio archive, concept work, FOMOLOGY client roster

**Client Delivery**:
A system or product website that was delivered to a real client and worked for its intended use, without implying that it remains live today or that every displayed feature reached production.
_Avoid_: Live Product, Production System, Prototype

**Demo Account**:
An owner-controlled test account used to demonstrate a delivered client system without exposing real customer records. Its visible product flows may be shown publicly, while confidential portfolio documents remain internal reference material.
_Avoid_: customer account, live customer data, production analytics

**Outcome Claim**:
A client-work result stated with a clear baseline, time window, and careful attribution. For Chat Bot, the approved public wording separates capacity (“รองรับงานที่เดิมต้องใช้แอดมิน 2–3 คน”) from the three-month client-reported revenue result (“ภายใน 3 เดือน รายได้เพิ่มขึ้นสูงสุดประมาณ 2 เท่า”) and never presents either as a guaranteed result.
_Avoid_: success metric, guaranteed result, unqualified growth claim

**Client Work Preview**:
A muted Cloudinary video preview of a Demo Account used as visual proof for Previous Client Work. Only the preview currently in the viewport may autoplay; reduced-motion users receive a poster, and selecting a preview opens the full video without navigating to a case-study page.
_Avoid_: decorative motion, four simultaneous autoplay videos, client-data recording

**Independent Work**:
Owner-created prototypes, concepts, exercises, editorial work, and experiments shown after Previous Client Work on Products. These items retain explicit maturity labels and are not presented as commissioned client deliveries.
_Avoid_: client case study, delivered system, customer work

**Production Service**:
The service in which a client hires FOMOLOGY to create an AI video for a defined project; the showcase does not present fixed packages or pricing.
_Avoid_: Lab generation, course purchase, credit top-up

**Production Client**:
A person or business that hires FOMOLOGY to produce an AI video rather than creating it inside the Lab.
_Avoid_: student, creator, Lab user

**Project Inquiry**:
A prospective client's conversation with FOMOLOGY about scoped AI-video, website, custom-system, or automation work.
_Avoid_: support ticket, course question, Lab job, checkout

**Contact Us**:
The explicitly named hiring action that leads to a Project Inquiry through the Fastwork Hiring Path, separate from course learning.
_Avoid_: course checkout, unlabeled external inquiry

**FOMOLOGY Lab**:
The future creation product where creators apply what they learned to a new idea by developing a Draft into a Final and optionally applying 4K Enhance. It does not process the completed First Win and is not required to reach one.
_Avoid_: course player, gallery, external generator

**Lab Project**:
A new creative work started in FOMOLOGY Lab after the creator has learned the path: New Idea → Draft → Final → optional 4K Enhance.
_Avoid_: First Win continuation, Course exercise

**Draft**:
A fast, lower-cost preview used to explore and choose a creative direction before committing to full quality.
_Avoid_: cheap video, test render

**Final**:
The full-quality FHD version rendered from an approved Draft while preserving its creative direction.
_Avoid_: upscale, Astra render

**4K Enhance**:
An optional premium transformation that adds detail to a Final and may creatively alter the source.
_Avoid_: Final, required upscale
